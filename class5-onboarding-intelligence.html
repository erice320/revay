What's Broken — 5 Issues Identified
1

GL Code Formatting Mismatch Between PSA and QBO

The PSA uses a dash-separated format (e.g., Asset General 1-1000-) while QuickBooks uses colon-separated parent/child notation. Class 5 imported GL codes from their old billing system with the wrong format, causing mapping failures. P&L reports and tax billing are blocked as a result.

"We need to know the format that it's supposed to look like in QuickBooks. Because I think in QuickBooks you use colons. And in QuickBooks, what's the formatting? Whereas if they give us a screenshot of what it generally looks like in QuickBooks, it would answer all of our questions."

Status: Halle committed to getting Andrew (Integrations team) on a call. That call had not yet happened as of Apr 8.

2

Product SKUs Cannot Be Edited After Creation

Ray discovered that SKUs (Product IDs) in the PSA are locked after creation — they cannot be edited. During early catalog setup, product names and SKUs were concatenated improperly, and now those errors are permanent. The only fix is deleting and recreating products, which is disruptive.

Impact: Product catalog inconsistencies are frozen in place. Class 5 has paused adding any new products until the existing issues are resolved.

Product question: Is this a product limitation or a configuration issue? If the former, this needs a product decision — either unlock SKU editing or provide a bulk re-import path.

3

Product Descriptions Not Transferring Into Quotes

Descriptions are not pulling correctly into quoting templates, forcing Ray to manually enter information on every quote. This is a separate issue from GL mapping but compounds the overall frustration. The upcoming quoting webinar was referenced as a potential help, but no concrete fix was committed.

4

Missing Tax Codes — Oregon Not Configured

Oregon geographic tax codes were not set up during initial configuration, causing incorrect tax application on some accounts. Ray flagged this as a long-term audit exposure. He was asked to use an exported item list to verify tax mapping and insert geographic codes, but no Rev.io team member has confirmed the correct codes.

5

ConnectWise API Integration Needs Attention Before Sunsetting

Class 5 has an active API integration with ConnectWise. ConnectWise is being discontinued (client's decision, not CW's), and this integration needs to be addressed before cutover. This is a secondary priority but adds urgency — if the PSA integration isn't complete before they shut off ConnectWise, there's a gap in their workflow.
