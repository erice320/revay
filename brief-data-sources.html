Methodology — How Briefs Were Built

Step 1 — Salesforce structured query: Pulled Opportunity (Name, Amount, CloseDate, Owner, Product_Type__c, Description, NextStep), Account (BillingCity/State, Industry, PSA_Platform__c), and Contact records for each deal via the Salesforce REST API. This covers all green-tagged fields above.

Step 2 — Email task extraction: Pulled Task records associated with each Opportunity (synced from Outreach). These contain the full email thread text — from these we extracted contact details in email signatures, confirmation of deal context (ISC West booth number, deferred billing language, kickoff promises), and discovery notes not structured in SF fields.

Step 3 — AI analysis: Interpreted the raw data to fill contextual fields: migration complexity tier, Canadian tax treatment (SureTax exemption, provincial GST/HST rates), kickoff deadline calculations against Brent's mandate, role-to-function mappings (Controller → billing contact, Field SE → mobile app user), and risk flags. These are clearly attributed as analysis in the briefs and this document.

What was NOT available: Kaia call transcripts were not available for these 4 deals at the time of generation (ISC West closes happened same-day; GuentherTech and Viking Lock had limited Outreach call history). The pipeline typically supplements SF data with Kaia transcript intelligence — for these deals, SF email tasks served as the primary unstructured data source.

Data gaps: Accounting software, GL codes, payment processor details, and full user lists are commonly missing from the sales record — especially for trade show closes. These are documented as gaps in each brief for the SA to collect at kickoff, rather than guessed at.
