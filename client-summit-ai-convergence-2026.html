What changes with agents

AI is evolving from assistant to operating layer.

Level 1

Prompt

Individual productivity. Useful, but hard to govern or monetize.

Level 2

Workflow

Repeatable tasks: summarize, classify, draft, route, reconcile.

Level 3

Agent

Multi-step execution with tools, memory, rules, and human checkpoints.

Level 4

System

Agents embedded into support, billing, security, customer ops, and analytics.

Level 5

Service

Packaged, priced, delivered, monitored, governed, and renewed.

Roadmap customers can actually follow

A 90-day path from curiosity to managed AI service.

Days 1–15
Find the wedge

Pick one painful, measurable workflow: ticket triage, dispatch, billing QA, customer summaries, security reporting, quote-to-cash handoffs.

Days 16–30
Put guardrails in place

AI policy, approved tools, data/access rules, human checkpoints, exception handling, and success metric.

Days 31–60
Automate the workflow

Deploy a narrow AI workflow inside PSA/RMM/billing/customer ops. Track time saved, quality, exceptions, and customer impact.

Days 61–90
Package and expand

Turn the workflow into a repeatable service package with pricing, onboarding checklist, support model, reporting, and renewal narrative.

The goal is not to “do AI.” The goal is to sell and operate a better business outcome.
