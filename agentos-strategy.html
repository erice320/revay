Executive Summary

The Case in 60 Seconds

The Opportunity

MSPs are the distribution layer for AI to 40,000+ SMBs who will never buy AI directly from hyperscalers. No platform exists today that lets MSPs build, sell, and bill AI agents to their end customers. That's the whitespace.

The Strategy

Two products. One platform. Revii manages the MSP's own business (internal AI). AgentHub lets MSPs deliver AI to their clients (external revenue). Same billing rails, same data fabric, compounding flywheel.

The Moat

Rev.io's billing DNA is the hardest layer to replicate. Every competitor can spin up inference endpoints. Almost none can meter heterogeneous AI consumption, reconcile it against MSP pricing tiers, and push payments through an integrated rail. We already do this.

⚠️ Critical Warning: The Infrastructure Margin Trap

The original plan proposed 20–40% infrastructure markup. This is NOT sustainable. Model API costs fall 70–80%/year. Cloudflare, Neon, and Railway are racing to zero. When Microsoft notices MSPs reselling AI, they will offer Copilot Studio + Azure AI with channel incentives that undercut any independent reseller.

The fix: Make margin on agent template IP and outcome billing — not compute. Consumption pass-through at 10–15% only (prevents route-around). Rev.io must be an agent IP company with billing infrastructure — not an infrastructure reseller that happens to have agents.

⚠️ MULTI-TENANCY IS THE #1 ENGINEERING PRIORITY — before any partner integrations begin.

MSP A's end customer data must never leak into MSP B's agent context. This requires namespace isolation at every layer: Neon (database branches per tenant), Qdrant (namespaces), model context (strict API key scoping per tenant). One data leakage event in a healthcare or finance vertical = company-ending event. Build it right before launch. No exceptions.

⚠️ The Tensions of Moving This Fast (be honest about them)

Technical debt is the price of admission. Month 1 multi-tenancy scoped to 3 tenants means hardcoded configs that need unwinding for Month 7+ scaling. Budget 30% of Month 7–8 engineering for this refactoring.

The onboarding bottleneck is real. Without self-service until Month 5 prototype, every MSP onboarding requires engineering time. At 5 MSPs it's manageable. At 10 it starts to break. This is why Month 4's exit gate is "PM can onboard without engineering hands-on."

The PSA dependency. Revenue Finder requires MSP to export billing and RMM data — we're not building a new PSA. This means onboarding is data-access-first, not self-service-first. Plan for 1–2 weeks of data wrangling per MSP in the early months.
Risk Assessment

Top Risks & Mitigations

ConnectWise or Kaseya builds customer-facing agent delivery natively
HIGH
They have 10× the install base and can ship features to existing customers without needing to win new platform adoption. This is the existential threat.
Speed + differentiation. They're building AI FOR MSPs. We build AI THROUGH MSPs to end customers. Own this distinction as a category. If ConnectWise ships the equivalent in Month 12, we need 200 MSPs already locked in. Time is the only mitigation.
Multi-tenancy failure causes data leakage between MSP tenants
HIGH — but preventable
One data leakage event where MSP A's client data appears in MSP B's agent context ends the product in regulated verticals (healthcare, finance, legal). This is a company-ending event.
Multi-tenancy architecture is Engineering Priority #1. Nothing ships until namespace isolation is validated at every layer. No shortcuts, no exceptions. Pre-launch security audit required.
Rewst expands into customer-facing agent delivery
MEDIUM-HIGH
$76M raised. Deep MSP relationships. Already the workflow automation leader. One strategic pivot away from being our direct competitor in the customer-facing space.
Move faster. Consider partnership — Rewst's MSP-internal workflows feed customer-facing AgentHub agents. If partnership is viable, this threat becomes an asset. Open partnership conversation now while Rewst is still workflow-focused, not agent-delivery-focused.
MSP channel lacks skills to sell AI to end customers
MEDIUM
Most MSPs sell break-fix and managed services. Selling AI agents to a law firm or dental practice requires a completely different consultative sales motion that most MSPs don't have.
Make it turnkey. Pre-build sales collateral, ROI calculators, and self-serve demo environments. Year 1: Rev.io co-sells with MSPs. Year 2: MSPs sell independently. The Revenue Finder demo sells itself — show $14,200 recovered in first scan. No sales skill required.
Partner stack becomes unmaintainable as we scale
MITIGATED
The original 50-partner plan would require 15–20 dedicated partnership engineers. Rev.io is ~200 people. API changes, startup failures, and pricing shifts would create constant maintenance debt.
Already mitigated by cutting to 14 partners. Phase 3 opens partner SDK — partners self-integrate. Never expose more than one vendor option per category to MSPs. Build abstraction layers so swaps don't require MSP-facing changes.
