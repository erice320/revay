Core leak
05 / 13

The largest leak is activity without a conversion path.

Too much generic motion

Many touches were calls, emails, Summit invites, or check-ins—not blocker-specific reactivation.

Too few hard outcomes

A meaningful meeting should end in a reopened opportunity, a future date, or a clear disqualification.

No release owner

Product shipped the unlock; no single operating cadence owned conversion from release to revenue.

The miss is not “no one followed up.” The miss is follow-up without enough control.

Management answer

Do not call a release commercially launched until the account motion is visible.

1. Target cohort
Accounts, MRR, blocker
2. Buyer proof
Demo, parity, caveats
3. Owner + play
Message, next step, deadline
4. Outcome
Opp, future date, or disqualification

Product should validate feature depth on the narrow repeat-capability list. GTM should own conversion discipline on the larger no-opportunity group.
