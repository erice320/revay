# Example Council Packet — Executive Offsite Prep

**Packet ID:** PKT-260528-OFFSITE-PREP  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Priority:** High  
**Decision Owner:** Evan Rice  
**Synthesizer:** Revay  
**Deadline:** TBD  

## Objective
Prepare a 360-degree executive offsite input packet that identifies the most important cross-functional themes, risks, tensions, and decisions Rev.io leadership should discuss.

## Business Context
Executive offsites are most valuable when they surface what is actually happening across the business, not just what each department reports upward. Department specialty agents can provide a fuller view by contributing source-backed signals from their domains.

## Scope
Contributors should focus on:
- Material business risks
- Cross-functional friction
- Customer-impacting patterns
- Product/process constraints
- Revenue and retention implications
- Decisions leadership should make
- Evidence that supports or weakens the signal

## Out of Scope
- Generic strategy advice
- Department self-promotion
- Unsourced claims
- Long-form debate
- Tasks that do not connect to executive-level decisions

## Required Contributors
- Sales/GTM agent
- Product agent
- Client Success / AM agent
- Support agent
- Onboarding agent
- Finance / Ops agent if available
- Revay synthesis

## Output Contract
Final synthesis should include:
- Top 5 executive offsite themes
- Evidence summary by theme
- Cross-functional tensions
- Decisions needed
- Suggested agenda sections
- Pre-read links
- Recommended owners for follow-up actions

## Evidence Requirements
Each contribution must include source systems, timestamps, links/record IDs when possible, retrieval status, and confidence.

## Close Condition
Packet is complete when required contributions are received or explicitly marked missing, Revay produces synthesis, Evan/delegated owner confirms decision/action outputs, and follow-up actions are captured.
