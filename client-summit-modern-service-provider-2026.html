Where AI becomes valuable

AI becomes valuable in the workflow.

Level 1

Personal use

Individual productivity. Useful. Hard to govern or support.

Level 2

Team workflow

Repeatable work: summarize, classify, draft, route, reconcile.

Level 3

Managed agent

Multi-step work with tools, rules, auditability, and approval points.

Level 4

Connected system

Tied to support, billing, security, customer operations, and reporting.

Level 5

Billable service

Packaged, priced, delivered, monitored, governed, and renewed.

The revenue opportunity starts when AI is supportable, repeatable, and billable.
From AI use to a managed service6
Walk the ladder quickly. Most of the room is at 1 or 2. The money is at 5. Do not linger on tools. The punchline is the commercial test: can you support it, repeat it, and bill it? If not, it is still a hobby inside the business — theirs or yours.
