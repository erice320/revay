Executive Thesis

Rev.io's constraint is not demand. It is the absence of a measured system that proves which sources, products, customer profiles, and sales motions convert into live, retained, margin-positive customers.

The strongest retrieved evidence points to won-to-live value realization, especially PSA, as the measured operating constraint. Source quality remains a major hypothesis until joined to onboarding, support, retention, expansion, and margin.
Board-Level Risk

Rev.io can create pipeline and bookings while still missing profitable growth.

The risk is scaling unproven cohorts before proving which motions become qualified, implementable, live, low-support, referenceable, retained-margin revenue.

Board-safe claim

Closed Won MRR is not the same as implemented, retained, profitable revenue.

Decision risk

Scaling demand before the conversion spine exists can reward volume while hiding value-realization and cost-to-serve failures.

Board Ask

Approve a 90-day Profitable Revenue Conversion Program.

Owned by Evan with Finance, RevOps, Sales, Marketing, Channel, Onboarding, Product, CS/Support, and Security.

The first decision is not "increase demand" or "ship more product." The first decision is to stand up a weekly retained-margin conversion spine, then use it to decide what to scale, narrow, fix, reprice, or stop.

Sequenced Outcomes

What changes by day 60 and day 90.

60 days
  • Enforce qualification gates by product motion.
  • Produce first retained-revenue bridges for key cohorts.
  • Classify top capability gaps into operating dispositions.
  • Build deal-ready trust proof using verified claims.
90 days
  • Reallocate effort toward cohorts with strongest evidence.
  • Narrow or reject low-fit demand earlier.
  • Run monthly revenue product review.
  • Tie reference eligibility to usage, support health, onboarding quality, renewal posture, and revenue tier.
Stop Doing

Stop rewarding volume before value is proven.

  • Treating raw opportunity creation as proof of growth.
  • Treating event volume or campaign activity as proof of profitable growth.
  • Treating Closed Won MRR as implemented revenue.
  • Mixing PSA, Billing, Payments, Channel, CommerceHub, add-ons, and Cyber Protect into one generic funnel.
  • Using roadmap volume as proof of strategy.
  • Using references without customer health, usage, support, renewal, and onboarding validation.
  • Making margin, CAC, payback, retention, expansion, or source ROI claims from partial data.
