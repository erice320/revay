The provider playbook

How providers get started: Discover → Sell → Buy/Build → Implement → Manage.

Play 1

Discover

Surface process debt. Map one painful workflow, data source, owner, cost, and measurable outcome.

Play 2

Sell

Sell the outcome, not the model. Price against actions, workflows, time saved, or business lift.

Play 3

Buy

Curate trusted agents and prebuilt workflows where speed matters more than custom IP.

Play 4

Build

Create reusable vertical or horizontal workflow IP where the client’s process is proprietary.

Play 5

Implement

Integrate with identity, data, PSA, billing, support, and approval paths. Train the team.

Play 6

Manage

Monitor, govern, tune, report ROI, handle exceptions, and expand to the next workflow.

Start small → ship fast → measure → expand. One credible workflow beats a broad AI menu.

What to package first

The first offers should be narrow, measurable, and close to workflows providers already know.

AI Readiness + Governance Audit

Fixed-fee assessment: tool inventory, policy gaps, data/access review, shadow AI exposure, first-workflow recommendation.

Best first sale when trust is high but AI maturity is low.

Workflow Automation Pilot

30–60 day pilot around one workflow: ticket triage, quote-to-cash handoff, billing QA, dispatch summaries, customer follow-up, or security reporting.

Best proof-of-value motion.

Managed Agent Operations

Monthly package: monitoring, exception handling, tuning, audit logs, policy updates, usage/cost controls, and ROI dashboard.

Best recurring revenue motion.

A practical 90-day starting point

Move from interest to a supportable AI service model.

Days 1–15
Choose the workflow

Pick one painful, measurable workflow: ticket triage, dispatch, billing QA, customer summaries, security reporting, quote-to-cash handoffs.

Days 16–30
Set the rules

AI policy, approved tools, data/access rules, human checkpoints, exception handling, and success metrics.

Days 31–60
Deploy narrowly

Deploy a narrow AI workflow inside PSA, RMM, billing, or customer operations. Track time saved, quality, exceptions, and customer impact.

Days 61–90
Package the service

Turn the workflow into a repeatable service package with pricing, onboarding checklist, support model, reporting, and renewal narrative.

The goal is not to have the longest AI menu. The goal is to make one valuable workflow safe, useful, billable, and supportable.
