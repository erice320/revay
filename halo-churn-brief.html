Systemic Patterns Why Halo displacements are failing — 6 root causes
⚡ Pattern Summary

This is not an account management problem. These are systemic failures that will repeat on every Halo displacement until they are fixed. Ravguard, GuentherTech, and Novalera share the same 6 failure modes. If we don't address these, winning from Halo is a churn factory, not a growth motion.

1. We're selling the roadmap
Affects: All 3 accounts
Halo delivers features on Day 1. We sell "Apollo 3, June." NinjaOne integration, two-way QB sync, multi-provider packages, product line-item descriptions — all sold as "coming." Halo customers are feature-parity buyers. We're asking them to pay now for something we'll build later.
2. Halo is the benchmark and we're losing on UX basics
Affects: GuentherTech, Novalera
Ben said "the way we did it with Halo" 3+ times across 4 calls. Tony said "Halo, despite its grievances, still works." Both customers left Halo for one specific reason — everything else Halo did adequately. We're not matching baseline on ticketing, QB sync, stability, or communication.
3. Onboarding on unstable foundations
Affects: Novalera (confirmed), likely Ravguard
Novalera kicked off Feb 20 while the PHP auth rewrite was 50–60% complete — a known cause of platform-wide slowness. GuentherTech had a 4-week ticket loading bug. Engineering and GTM are not coordinating on which customers can safely begin live configuration.
4. No escalation triggers in onboarding
Affects: All 3 accounts
Both active accounts had Week 1 issues. Both still had the same issues at Week 4–8. There is no mechanism that fires when an SA-level issue can't be resolved in 5 business days. Nicole is carrying SA + CSM + escalation coordinator on multiple at-risk accounts simultaneously.
5. Cross-selling before stabilizing
Affects: GuentherTech, Novalera
GuentherTech was pitched Payments AR 4 days after PSA close. Novalera's Payments AR was attempted and dropped to "Price." Both customers interpret early cross-sell as tone-deaf when their core product isn't working. The AR team is operating on its own timeline independent of onboarding health.
6. Communication gaps compound technical failures
Affects: Novalera (explicitly), likely others
Novalera: no status page, broken community forum (couldn't file feature requests), no proactive outage comms. An outage you warn customers about is frustrating. An outage you don't warn about is a trust breach. This is fixable infrastructure Rev.io hasn't built yet — and it's the exact pain point Halo customers are escaping.
