Honest Assessment

What We Should Measure Better

The ROI case is strong, but here's where we're being honest with ourselves.

✅

What We Can Quantify

Time savings on automated tasks (hours × blended rate). Infrastructure built vs. consultant quote. Systems running 24/7 without headcount. Data that didn't exist before (612 transcripts analyzed, 110 onboarding projects tracked).

⚠️

What's Harder to Prove (Yet)

Revenue directly attributed to an insight from a brief. Specific deal won because of Kaia intelligence. MRR retained because of an onboarding alert. We need a feedback loop — when a rep acts on a Revay insight, we should tag it.

🔧

Cost Optimization Opportunities

Move some crons to Haiku (cheaper) instead of Sonnet. Tighten context on long-running sessions. Simon/Raisebot's large context writes ($2,659) suggest a different use pattern — may be worth reviewing. Projected: 20–30% cost reduction possible.

📐

The Right Tracking Framework

Proposed: tag Salesforce opportunities when reps cite Revay intel. Track onboarding accounts that received risk alerts vs. those that didn't. Measure time-to-action on competitive signals. 60-day data will tell a cleaner story.

Recommendation: Establish a simple attribution tagging system in SF for the next 60 days. When a rep closes a deal or prevents churn using Revay intelligence, log it. By Q3 board meeting, we'll have direct revenue attribution, not just proxy metrics.
