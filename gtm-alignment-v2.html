Revay's Fresh Strategic Plays

Five bold, specific, immediately actionable growth moves — synthesized from 90 days of SF data, 100 Kaia calls, 74 onboarding transcripts, and a full loss anatomy. These go beyond the March plan.

01
The "Contract Clock" CW Displacement Campaign
ConnectWise accounts renew on annual cycles — predominantly May–June for calendar-year SMBs. With 13 open CW pipeline deals + 5 Kaia mentions in the last 30 days, the timing window is NOW. Run a targeted outreach sequence to every open CW deal: "Your contract is likely up in 60 days. Here's a comparison of what you'd be paying vs. Rev.io PSA — and a 30-day free migration offer." Add urgency with a migration incentive (free onboarding fee through June 30). This is the highest-leverage timing play in the entire pipeline right now.
💰 Potential: 5–8 additional CW closes in Q2 · Owner: Ryan Koontz / Jennifer Hopkins
02
The "Apollo 2 Is Live — You Were Right" Re-Engagement Blitz
There are 133 documented feature-gap losses — 98 of which are unlocked by Apollo 2 features that shipped in March/April. Reps don't know what shipped. Prospects don't know either. Build a 1-week blitz: identify every "Software Missing Capabilities" loss from the last 90 days, map the specific feature they wanted to what shipped in Apollo 2, and send a highly personalized one-line email: "The [specific feature] you needed last time shipped on [date]. I'd love to show you — 15 minutes?" This is the highest-ROI sales motion available because the prospect was already qualified and engaged. Target the top 25 by MRR first.
💰 Potential: $15–25K MRR recovered · Owner: Sales + Marketing · Timeline: This week
03
The "Billing → PSA" Velocity Motion
Rev.io already has deep trust with its Billing customer base — and those customers are exactly the IT service providers who need PSA. But the data shows: Billing clients who buy PSA are our highest-risk onboarding failures (Pattern #8 from the 74-transcript analysis). The root cause: they expect CW-level polish and have zero tolerance for gaps because they're already relying on Rev.io for billing. The play: don't abandon the Billing→PSA cross-sell motion, but create a separate, higher-touch onboarding track for Billing-to-PSA converts. Assign senior SAs, set explicit expectations during sales ("This will take 90 days to stabilize"), and include a 30-day check-in call with a VP. This converts your highest-risk segment into your most profitable expansion vector.
💰 Potential: Recover GuentherTech, prevent next 2-3 Billing→PSA churns · Owner: Sales + Onboarding
04
The "Justin Lee Playbook" Fast-Follow Program
Justin Lee is closing at 39.1% — more than 3× Patrick Davies (11.7%) and nearly 7× Husam Zalmiyar (5.6%). This is not random. Lee is doing something structurally different in qualification, discovery framing, or follow-up cadence. Then build a 1-hour training session where Lee walks the team through his process. Simultaneously, do the same with Ingrid Beard (22 wins, 36.1% rate) — she may be using a different but equally effective approach. This is the fastest, cheapest rep performance lever available. Target outcome: bring the bottom 4 reps from 6-11% to 15%+ within 60 days.

05
The "Stage 2 → 3 Bridge" Conversion Fix
Stage 1 no-shows are improving (20% and falling). But there's a second graveyard nobody's talking about: 708 deals that completed discovery and then died. These prospects were qualified enough to show up, engaged enough to talk for 20+ minutes — and then went silent. This is where the product demo isn't converting. The fix: audit the Stage 2 → 3 handoff. Are reps asking "what would it take to see the full product?" at end of discovery? Are they booking the demo before leaving the call? Implement a rule: no opportunity can exit Stage 2 without a scheduled Stage 3 date on the calendar. Add a "Stage 2 Close Question" script: "Based on what you told me, I think [specific use case] is where the product will land for you. I want to show you exactly that — can we do 30 minutes Thursday?" This single habit change could unlock 10–15% more pipeline progression monthly.
💰 Potential: 15–20% conversion lift from discovery pool · Owner: Sales Mgmt · Timeline: This sprint

Build this as a structured play with a 90-day expansion window. Graduation = the start of the upsell motion, not the end of the relationship. Target: 2 expansions per graduation cohort = ~$1,500–3,000 MRR from graduating accounts monthly.
