Root cause
06 / 13

“Available” never became a named win-back operating system.

No named cohort per release

Roadmap records do not carry the account list, expected MRR, seller owner, and launch action.

No buyer-readiness gate

Delivered does not prove parity, demo quality, sales proof, pricing clarity, or support readiness.

No opp creation trigger

If a prior lost account meets after the blocker ships, that should create a tracked reactivation opp or a disposition reason.

Generic activity pollutes signal

Many touches are broad marketing, Summit, no-answer calls, or generic Outreach—not original-blocker reactivation.

Second losses change reasons

Timing, no-show, decision-maker, contract, competitor and price dominate over repeat software capability loss.

Attribution gap

Roadmap, SF activity, opps, adoption and onboarding are not joined into one release-to-MRR ledger.

What to change
10 / 13

Install a release-to-revenue control loop.

1. Revenue thesis
MRR, accounts, blocker
2. Buyer-ready proof
Demo, parity, caveats
3. Targeted activation
Owner, message, deadline
4. Closed-loop outcome
Opp, win/loss, why

No priority release should be called commercially launched until all four gates are visible.

30-day sprint
11 / 13

Recommended immediate operating plan

Top 25 reactivation accounts

Product + Sales approve blocker resolved, proof available, seller owner, exec sponsor if needed, and do-not-touch caveats.

Reopen/disposition rule

Every meaningful re-engagement creates a reopened opp or a structured disposition: no response, no-show, contract, price, fit, competitor, same blocker, new blocker.

Release-specific outreach

Touches must reference the original blocker and what changed. Generic Summit/newsletter touches do not count as win-back motion.

Weekly conversion review

Target MRR → contacted → replied → met → reopened pipeline → won/lost by reason.
