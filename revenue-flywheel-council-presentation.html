What Not To Claim

These claims are not board-ready yet.

Do not overstate ROI

  • No paid-channel ROI, CAC, payback, or channel reallocation until paid activity, campaign/source fields, outcomes, retained revenue, and margin are joined.
  • No Salesforce Amount or broad MRR/ARR claims without RevOps/Finance field-semantics confirmation.

Do not overstate causality

  • ICP discipline is plausible, not proven as the single binding constraint.
  • Product gaps alone did not cause PSA underperformance without feature-specific ARR exposure and implementation causality.
  • Revin's view is absent and should not be inferred.
Recommended Board Narrative

Rev.io's most evidenced near-term growth constraint is profitable value realization: selecting the right opportunities, proving the product and onboarding path, delivering roadmap and security proof for higher-quality buyers, and instrumenting the funnel so leadership can see which motions create retained gross profit.

Operating Model

Fix the flywheel as a connected system.

1. SelectRight opportunities, segments, buyer readiness, and decision process.
2. ProveProduct proof, roadmap confidence, security evidence, and referenceable accounts.
3. RealizeOnboarding, support root-cause reduction, account health, and retained value.
4. InstrumentSource to outcome to retained margin, with Finance and RevOps definitions.

The flywheel does not accelerate cleanly until value realization and measurement are connected.

30-Day Action Set

Five moves to put in motion now.

1. Revenue Evidence Ledger

Join source/campaign to opportunity outcome, product/segment, onboarding status, support friction, retained revenue, and margin.

2. PSA Value Realization tiger team

Rank canceled/functionality, RTS, on-hold, at-risk accounts, missing-capability losses, support themes, and roadmap blockers by exposure.

3. Freeze broad ROI-based paid expansion

Continue tactical campaigns, but do not tell the board channel ROI is known.

4. De-risk product proof surfaces

Identity/access, onboarding-critical roadmap items, PSA integration/functionality proof, SOC 2 readiness, and referenceable account health.

5. Validate only the missing joins

Next pass should be evidence retrieval, not another opinion round.
