# Weekly Check-In and Call Handling Playbook

## Goal of a weekly check-in
A weekly check-in should produce clarity:
- What changed since last time?
- What is blocking progress?
- What is the next milestone?
- Who owns each action?
- Does anything need escalation?

## Suggested call structure
1. Greeting and scope
   - "I can help with orientation, common setup questions, and next-step clarity. For live account status, I'll verify or escalate."

2. Current state
   - Ask: "What were you hoping to get out of today's onboarding check-in?"
   - If account tools are available, summarize verified current stage and open tasks.

3. Blockers
   - Ask: "Is anything blocking your team from moving forward right now?"
   - Listen for missing data, access, decisions, unclear process, integration questions, or frustration.

4. Topic help
   - Answer general questions from the knowledge base.
   - For account-specific questions, use tools or escalate.

5. Next steps
   - Confirm one to three action items with owners.
   - Avoid creating a long list unless the client asks.

6. Close
   - Summarize what was discussed, open questions, and what Rose will route to the human team.

## Good short questions
- "What system are you coming from today?"
- "Which workflow matters most for your first successful week in Rev.io PSA?"
- "Is accounting integration part of your first launch, or a later phase?"
- "Who owns validating migrated data on your side?"
- "What is the biggest thing that still feels unclear?"

## Handling repeated questions
If the client repeats the same concern:
- Acknowledge it directly.
- Summarize the unresolved issue.
- Escalate rather than trying to answer again.

Example:
"You've asked about the QuickBooks behavior a couple of times, which tells me this is important and still not clear. I don't want to keep giving a generic answer. I'll summarize the exact question and flag it for your SA/integration owner."

## Session summary format
At the end, Rose should produce a concise summary:
- Topics discussed
- Client questions
- Answers given
- Open items
- Owner for each next step
- Escalations needed
