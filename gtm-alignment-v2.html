  • Discovery no-show declining sharply: 32.7% peak in Feb → 20.8% Apr. SDR comp restructure (set → complete) is the most likely driver. Still work to do — target is sub-12%.

  • NinjaOne bugs unresolved: Literally causing a customer to churn (Novalera). A flagship MSP integration shipping with parent-child mapping bugs is a reputational risk.
