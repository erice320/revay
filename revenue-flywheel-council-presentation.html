Story

Product to GTM to Onboarding to Support lifecycle, with actions tied to profitable growth.

Executive Thesis

Rev.io's problem is not that we cannot sell. It is that too much of what we sell does not yet become revenue we keep.

We are creating pipeline and closing deals, but bookings are running below plan, and the strongest measured leak is after the win: PSA implementations stall or cancel before they become live, durable customers.

Demand And GTM

We create pipeline, but lose too much early.

Success

Demand volume exists: 683 marketing-sourced leads, 423 marketing-created opportunities, 62 Closed Won totaling $44,128, plus 467 channel/partner events.

Challenge

Closed Lost clustered before or at demo: 156 Discovery Scheduled, 86 Discovery Completed, 71 Initial Product Demo. Loss patterns point to timing, proof, decision-maker, product-fit, price, and non-response issues.

Action

Build a weekly source/product/ICP bookings bridge to shift from more pipeline to more qualified, provable, implementable pipeline.

The Win

Closed Won is progress, not money in the bank.

Success

Rev.io is winning PSA/PSA 2.0 business: 173 Closed Won PSA/PSA 2.0 opportunities totaling $119,044 MRR since 2025-07-01. PSA 2.0 produced $6,300 of $6,461 May closed-won marketing MRR.

Challenge

Bookings are below plan: April closed-won bookings were $20,059 MRR versus $60,074.75 budget MRR; May-to-date was $10,850 versus $62,725.50 budget MRR.

Action

Report wins by product motion and lifecycle status. Do not treat booked MRR as implemented, retained, profitable revenue.

Onboarding Value Realization

The strongest measured leak is after the win.

Success

Close-to-kickoff is not the main measured problem: median 7 days, p75 13 days.

Challenge

Kickoff-to-graduation median is 79 days, p75 134, with only 35 graduation records. There are 98 active records lacking graduation, 29 return-to-sales/on-hold, and 36 missing milestones. PSA Web cancellations represent $37,379 booked MRR / $448,548 ARR.

Action

Launch a PSA Rescue and Prevention Board with owner, blocker, exposure, save/close/qualify-out decision, next action, due date, and escalation.

Support, Adoption, And Trust

Complexity lands in Support when it is not prevented upstream.

Success

Usage exists: Pendo critique retrieval found 125 accounts, 152,468 events, 82 strategic adopters, and 22 newly tracked accounts.

Challenge

Billing and PSA support pressure is material: Billing last 30 days 701, Billing unsolved 201; PSA last 30 days 270, PSA unsolved 99. SOC 2 also remains a trust gate: 177 of 208 controls Not Verified.

Action

Create a Revenue Friction Watchlist and deal-ready verified trust packet. Define reference readiness using usage, support health, onboarding quality, renewal posture, and customer outcome.

Product As Multiplier

Product activity exists. The revenue linkage does not.

Success

Product work is happening: 704 roadmap records with 262 Delivered and 67 On Track; Pod Dashboard critique pass showed 490 features, 278 available, and 42 in progress.

Challenge

Visible roadmap relations to customer, onboarding, and feedback links returned zero linked records. Repeated missing-capability themes appear in PSA/PSA 2.0 losses, but revenue prioritization still needs the join.

Action

Build a Product Revenue Evidence Ledger for the top 25 PSA/value-realization blockers, classified as sell-around, qualify-out, roadmap-commit, workaround, enablement fix, or stop-selling-until-fixed.

By Day 90

What leaders should be able to decide.

  • Which product promises we can confidently sell.
  • Which sources and product motions create qualified, implementable opportunities.
  • Which deals should be qualified out before they hurt onboarding and support.
  • Which implementation blockers need product, enablement, pricing, or process intervention.
  • Which customers are healthy enough to become references.
  • Which motions are creating retained, profitable revenue or the best available cost-to-serve proxy.
Data Integrity

What not to overclaim.

  • CAC or payback.
  • Source ROI.
  • Customer-level gross margin.
  • Retention, churn, or expansion causality.
  • Product feature count as revenue impact.
  • Support ticket count as revenue impact.
  • Salesforce Amount/MRR totals without field-definition confirmation.

The recommendation does not depend on those claims. It depends on the visible lifecycle pattern and the need to manage it directly.

Board Ask

Approve a 90-day Product-to-GTM-to-Onboarding-to-Support lifecycle program.

The board is not being asked to approve a premature budget answer. It is being asked to approve the operating system that will show what to scale, fix, narrow, reprice, qualify out, or stop.

Closing

Scale what can be sold, implemented, supported, referenced, and retained profitably.

The growth engine has raw material: demand, product activity, wins, onboarding starts, usage, support signals, and customer proof opportunities. Everything that does not convert should be fixed, narrowed, repriced, qualified out, or stopped.
