01 · Overview

What is Summit

Rev.io Summit is the company's flagship annual customer conference: three days of general sessions, breakout tracks, an expo hall, and networking events for Rev.io customers and partners.

The basics

  • When: September 1–3, 2026 (Tue–Thu)
  • Where: Renaissance Atlanta Waverly Hotel & Convention Center, Atlanta, GA
  • Who attends: Rev.io customers and partners: telecom/CSPs, MSPs, and field service integrators, from C-suite to billing ops. Roughly half of this year's registrants are first-timers
  • Dress code: business casual
  • One ticket covers everything: all sessions, all receptions (including Braves Stadium), and all meals

Shape of the event

  • Day 1 (Tue): sponsor load-in and room setup, registration 1:00–5:00p, Welcome Reception & Expo 5:00–7:00p, VIP Dinner at C&S Oyster House 7:30–9:00p (Premium tickets)
  • Day 2 (Wed): the big day. General session morning (keynotes, industry panel, Jay McBain), breakout tracks A/B/C all afternoon, Revii Showcase, then the Networking Reception at Braves Stadium 6:30–10:00p
  • Day 3 (Thu): awards, product roadmap, breakouts, closing at 3:15p with the 2027 sign-up push
  • Agenda was built on a 7-track framework reviewed by an eight-person executive committee; sessions run in General Session (GB 3&4), Track A (GB 1), Track B (GB 2), and Track C (Wilton)
