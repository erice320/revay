Operating recommendations

  1. Change forecast review from % complete to template-normalized progress: completed/total, template type, comparable yes/no.
  2. Do not let legacy 5-step or missing milestone projects remain forecast-healthy without explicit owner confirmation and call-stage evidence.
  3. Use Fundamentals/Pre-Graduation/Graduated call evidence as the readiness gate; Configuration-heavy accounts stay intervention/recovery by default.
  4. Add a dashboard field: Milestone Template Cohort and stop mixing legacy milestones with new checklist in executive forecast views.
  5. Require actual graduation date hygiene for any project moved to Graduated.
