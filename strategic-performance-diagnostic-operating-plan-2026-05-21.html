Root-cause map

The operating problem is cross-functional, not departmental.

GTM

  • Low PSA conversion despite real market permission.
  • No-show / timing / decision-maker losses indicate weak process control.
  • Competitive wedge exists but is not yet dominant enough.

Product

  • Core workflow gaps show up repeatedly: QBO, dispatch, scheduling, mobile, inventory, portal.
  • Roadmap has many items; market needs fewer things done completely.
  • Revii can differentiate, but cannot compensate for core workflow instability.

Delivery

  • Onboarding forecast credibility is low.
  • Configuration dominates May call evidence.
  • Support is absorbing downstream friction instead of eliminating root causes.
Executive principle: Stop treating these as separate departmental misses. The client experiences one Rev.io system. The operating plan should be organized around client outcomes.

Executive operating priorities

Recommended executive priorities for the rest of 2026

1. PSA growth recovery room

Weekly executive review of PSA pipeline, losses, product blockers, and onboarding outcomes until conversion/pacing stabilizes.

Owner: Sales + Product + CS, not Sales alone.

2. Onboarding graduation command center

Turn every forecasted graduation into a named recovery plan: owner, blocker, next client commitment, exec escalation, real date.

First target: May/June PSA forecast accuracy and actual graduation rate.

3. Product “must be elegant” list

Concentrate roadmap calories on QBO/accounting, dispatch/scheduling, mobile tech workflow, inventory, portal, and migration automation.

Do not let low-evidence roadmap items crowd out loss-backed work.

4. Support root-cause loop

Every high-volume ticket theme should map to product fix, onboarding training, knowledge article, or account intervention.

Measure ticket prevention, not just response.

5. GTM qualification discipline

Shift from demo volume to controlled mutual evaluation: business issue, decision maker, current platform, contract timing, success criteria.

6. Attach motion reset

Payments and Cyber/CommerceHub need an explicit attach model or lower expectations. Current attainment says they are not naturally attaching at plan level.

How to run the conversation

How to make this productive, not performative

  1. Start with mission: we have a real market opportunity and clients need what we are trying to become.
  2. Then show the truth: bookings and onboarding data say we are not executing to the level of the opportunity.
  3. Make it systemic: this is not “Sales missed” or “Product missed” or “Support missed.” It is a connected client journey problem.
  4. Force priority: the company cannot do every roadmap/GTM/support initiative at equal intensity.
  5. End with ownership: 3–5 executive priorities, each with metric, owner, cadence, and 30-day proof point.
Recommended tone: board-level candor, executive ownership, and practical next steps. “We should be optimistic about the opportunity and unsatisfied with our current performance.”

Executive decisions needed

The meeting should produce decisions, not just alignment.

Decide what gets executive gravity

  • Is PSA 2.0 the primary H2 growth recovery motion?
  • Which product gaps are company-level priorities vs normal roadmap items?
  • What onboarding outcomes are unacceptable by end of June?

Decide what gets deprioritized

  • Which roadmap/workflow items stop consuming focus until core workflow reliability improves?
  • Which GTM motions are underperforming enough to pause or redesign?
  • Which metrics are vanity vs true operating health?

Decide ownership

  • One accountable executive per priority.
  • Weekly operating cadence through Q2 exit.
  • Clear escalation path for product/onboarding/support blockers.

Decide proof points

  • 30-day PSA conversion proof.
  • 30-day onboarding graduation proof.
  • 30-day top-ticket-theme reduction proof.
  • Updated H2 forecast from actual conversion, not aspiration.
