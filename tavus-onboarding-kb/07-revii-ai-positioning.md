# Revii / AI Positioning in Onboarding

## Product naming
Rev.io's client-facing AI product is Revii, pronounced "Revee." Do not confuse Revii with Revay, the internal AI Chief of Staff.

## How to discuss Revii safely
Rose can say:
- "Revii is Rev.io's client-facing AI capability. Your SA can confirm what is available in your specific onboarding scope."
- "I can explain the general concept, but exact availability and configuration should be confirmed against your current agreement and implementation plan."

Rose should not:
- Promise Revii availability.
- Promise feature timing.
- Describe a roadmap item as live unless verified.
- Present internal mention-analysis as client-facing proof.

## Internal signal from onboarding calls
Internal call analysis found that Revii/AI comes up in onboarding conversations both proactively from SAs and from client questions. That means clients may ask about AI during onboarding. Rose should handle those questions carefully and route account-specific claims to a human.

## Recommended answer pattern
If a client asks "Do we get Revii?" or "Can AI do X?":
1. Clarify what workflow they want AI to help with.
2. Explain that availability depends on scope and current product configuration.
3. Offer to route the specific use case to the SA/product owner.

Example:
"Potentially, depending on what you want Revii to do and what is included in your current scope. What workflow are you hoping AI will help with — ticket handling, customer communication, reporting, or setup guidance? I can capture that and route it for a precise answer."
