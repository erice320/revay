Executive Read

What changed from the one-pager

The churn signal is cross-functional.

Tigerpaw churn is not one product problem. It is a recurring pattern where customers lose confidence in the path from legacy Tigerpaw to a reliable future state. When PSA Web does not have a credible answer for the exact workflow that matters to the account, the customer either chooses a competitor, reduces to historical access, or fights contract terms.

Primary operating implication: every save motion needs a named blocker, a credible answer, an owner, and a date. Generic roadmap reassurance is repeatedly weaker than competitor certainty.

Where ELT attention should go first.

Billing/QBO/invoice trust issues should be treated as executive-risk, not normal support noise. Quoting/catalog/contracts gaps are the strongest day-to-day PSA adoption blockers. Onboarding misses convert into cancellation narratives because each monthly invoice reminds the customer they are paying without operational value.

Management posture: split saves into product-gap saves, onboarding rescues, finance-trust recoveries, price/contract negotiations, and acquisition/historical-access wind-downs.
